Hi, I am Radit.

I am a Grade 12 student in Indonesia. I build embedded systems and I want to study Electrical Engineering, with a focus on power electronics.

My main project is PiezoRail. It is a prototype that tries to power a railway sensor node from the vibration of passing trains. I designed the hardware, wrote the firmware, and built the dashboard myself. This site tells the story of that project, including what failed and what I still do not know.

Read the PiezoRail case study About me

PiezoRail in short

Piezoelectric pads turn train vibration into electricity. The electricity charges a supercapacitor. That powers a small ESP32 node that measures vibration and temperature and sends the data to a receiver and a dashboard.

Status: working prototype. It has not been installed on a real railway.

Team Perzolex Tech, 7 students. I am the Lead Engineer and Team Lead.
