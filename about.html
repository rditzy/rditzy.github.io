About me

I am Ida Bagus Wididharma Raditya Putra Sanjaya. Everyone calls me Radit.

School

I am in Grade 12 at SMA Taruna Nusantara, Malang campus. It is a semi-military boarding school in Indonesia, so my days follow a fixed timetable. I built PiezoRail in my free hours inside that timetable.

What I do at school

  • PMR (Youth Red Cross) secretary, since Grade 10. I am the first person to hold this role on the Malang campus. I write permit letters and report the budget and activities to school management. I also adapt practices from the Magelang campus. The unit has 14 members.
  • KIR and Robotics. I lead the PiezoRail project under this club.
  • Olympiads. I took part in the EKKA 2026 national AI olympiad (I did not pass the pre-selection round). I won a bronze medal at IYCO, a chemistry olympiad.

What I work with

Embedded systems with ESP32, sensors and signal processing (such as FFT), Python, and PCB design concepts. I also enjoy physics and I am interested in renewable energy.

Why Electrical Engineering

PiezoRail showed me that I enjoy the point where a physics idea becomes a working circuit. It also showed me what I do not know yet: how to move energy efficiently from a source to a load. That is power electronics, and it is what I want to study. Then I want to bring it back to Indonesia, to railway monitoring with PT KAI.

I will be the first person in my family to study abroad.
